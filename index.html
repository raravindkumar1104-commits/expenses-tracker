      How the price is worked out

      The India estimate is the international spot price in rupees, plus import duty and GST. Since 13 May 2026 that is 15% customs duty and 3% IGST, about 18.45% in total. Jewellers add making charges on top, and city rates differ slightly. International spot is the price before any Indian taxes.
